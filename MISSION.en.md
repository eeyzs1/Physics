# Learning Goals

The user wants a Chinese HTML textbook covering every direction of physics, taking learners step by step from the introductory level to the ability to do independent research. The degree of completion of the catalog, of the lesson text, and of the research trainings must be kept distinct.

Currently executing: expanding the fields and the tiered topic lessons, and adding research replication trainings; "mastery" is not treated as a stage that can be conferred by reading alone.

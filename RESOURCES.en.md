# Textbook and Method Sources

Access date: 2026-11-01. The lesson text and exercises are original writing; the following are entry points for cross-checking and further reading, not sources for copying textbook passages wholesale.

- [OpenStax University Physics Vol. 1: Mechanics, waves, and sound](https://openstax.org/details/books/university-physics-volume-1)
- [OpenStax University Physics Vol. 2: Heat and electromagnetism](https://openstax.org/details/books/university-physics-volume-2)
- [OpenStax University Physics Vol. 3: Optics and modern physics](https://openstax.org/details/books/university-physics-volume-3)
- [CERN: The Standard Model](https://home.cern/science/physics/standard-model)
- [NASA Science: Universe](https://science.nasa.gov/universe/)
- [Caltech: The Feynman Lectures on Physics (advanced reading)](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/)
- [MIT OpenCourseWare: Physics courses](https://ocw.mit.edu/search/?d=Physics)

Teaching method: [teach SKILL.md](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/productivity/teach/SKILL.md). It uses short lessons, goals, feedback, retrieval practice, and spaced review; installing that skill is not required. Skill-directory search entry point: [skills.sh](https://skills.sh/).

OpenStax works well as the main textbook; the Feynman Lectures serve for a second pass at understanding, at a higher level of difficulty; CERN and NASA are used for overviews of research objects and fields. For advanced directions, the prerequisites and the route that follows matter more than rushing to memorize formulas.

The newly added content consists of original derivations and training designs; the entry-point links do not imply that every page has received item-by-item review by domain experts. When carrying out frontier projects, the specific literature must be re-checked at implementation time.

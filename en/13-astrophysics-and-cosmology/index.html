Stage-by-stage requirements

  1. Introductory: explain concepts qualitatively, put units on a consistent footing, and solve simple model problems.
  2. Undergraduate core: study the field's governing equations and experiments systematically, completing multi-chapter derivations and problem sets; the mini-lessons in this directory are only demonstrations of selected topics.
  3. Advanced: fill in the mathematics and specialized tools, read the corresponding graduate textbooks, and reproduce typical models; topics not yet written remain listed as to be added.
  4. Research training: reproduce results, verify the error budget, read papers, and accept review; only then gradually attempt original problems.

Systematic textbook: David Tong: cosmology. For cross-field prerequisites and milestones see the complete graded path.

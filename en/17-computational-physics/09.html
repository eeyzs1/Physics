Metropolis importance sampling

Goal: build the model by following the derivation, compute a new case independently, and point out when the approximation breaks down.

Prerequisites: the Monte Carlo lessons; the Boltzmann distribution. If the prerequisites feel shaky, first return to Mathematics preparation. The two intro lessons in this field do not by themselves satisfy every advanced prerequisite.

Problem and physical picture

The Metropolis algorithm samples according to Boltzmann weights via a propose-accept random walk: the acceptance probability min(1, e^(−βΔE)) satisfies detailed balance, so samples from a long chain are equivalent to the canonical ensemble — the workhorse engine of statistical-physics simulations.

acceptance probability α=min(1, e^(−βΔE))

From assumptions to results

  1. Propose a random perturbation: always accept energy-decreasing moves, accept energy-increasing moves with probability e^(−βΔE).
  2. Detailed balance guarantees that the stationary distribution is exactly the Boltzmann distribution.
  3. Step-to-step correlations in the chain make the effective sample size ≪ the number of steps (autocorrelation-time correction).
  4. With symmetric proposals the acceptance rate depends only on ΔE; there is no need to compute the partition function.

Please fill in the intermediate algebra on paper. If you cannot reproduce a step, first point to the equation where you are stuck, then ask me.

Worked example and check

Near the critical temperature, a 32×32 two-dimensional Ising model with Metropolis gives magnetization fluctuations and estimates of critical exponents; ensemble generation for protein folding and lattice QCD shares the same origin; empirically, tuning the acceptance rate to 30–50% works well.

Limits of applicability

The Markov chain must be ergodic and long enough; critical slowing down requires cluster algorithms (Wolff/Swendsen–Wang).

Transfer and counterexample

Why doesn't Metropolis need to compute the partition function?

Transfer-problem solution for comparison

The acceptance rate depends only on the energy difference; the partition function cancels in the ratio — which is precisely the quantity that cannot be computed directly.

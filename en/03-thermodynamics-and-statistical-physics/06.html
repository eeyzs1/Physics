Thermodynamic Potentials and Maxwell Relations

Goal: follow the derivation to build the model, independently compute a new case, and point out when the approximation fails.

Prerequisites: entropy; partial derivatives. If the prerequisites are unfamiliar, first return to Mathematics preparation. The two introductory lessons in this field do not amount to satisfying all advanced prerequisites.

The problem and the physical picture

Different experimental conditions call for different potentials: at fixed temperature and volume use the Helmholtz free energy F; at fixed temperature and pressure use the Gibbs free energy G. Mixed partial derivatives can be interchanged, giving the Maxwell relations, which replace hard-to-measure quantities with measurable ones.

F=U−TS; dF=−S dT−p dV; (∂S/∂V)T=(∂p/∂T)V

From assumptions to results

  1. A Legendre transform exchanges T dS for S dT to obtain F.
  2. Taking the mixed partial derivatives of dF in the two orders and setting them equal yields the Maxwell relations.

Fill in the intermediate algebraic steps on paper. If you cannot reproduce one, first point out the equation where you got stuck, then ask me about it.

Example and check

Substituting the ideal-gas p=NkT/V gives (∂p/∂T)V=Nk/V=(∂S/∂V)T; integrating yields the isothermal entropy change Nk ln(V₂/V₁), consistent with the direct calculation.

Range of applicability

Applies only to equilibrium states; small systems and nonequilibrium processes require extensions such as stochastic thermodynamics.

Independent calculation

Show solution

ΔS=R ln2=8.31×0.693=5.76 J/K.

Transfer and counterexample

2 mol of an ideal gas expands isothermally to 4 times its volume; what is the entropy change in J/K?

Check the transfer-problem solution

ΔS=2R ln4=2×8.31×1.386≈23.0 J/K.

Advanced self-test

One step more than the previous problem, or a unit conversion is needed; on review days 1, 3, and 7, switch to this problem to avoid memorizing the answer.

Show solution

At constant temperature, ΔF=−nRT·ln2=−8.314×300×0.693≈−1729 J.

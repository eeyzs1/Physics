Taxonomy map and coverage boundaries

Fields cannot be captured in a single
mutually exclusive list.

The local navigation holds 25 study fields plus the common preparation, 369 topic entries in all (names may repeat across fields). This is a fairly broad learning map, and it still does not claim to encompass every classification of physics.

Use four perspectives at the same time

  1. Foundational theory: classical mechanics, electromagnetism, thermodynamics and statistical physics, quantum mechanics, and relativity.
  2. Objects and scales of study: atoms, nuclei, particles, condensed matter, plasmas, Earth, and the universe.
  3. Methods: theory, mathematics, computation, experiment, observation, and data analysis; these methods run through every field.
  4. Applications and related disciplines: medicine, chemistry, energy, information, history, and education; they are not all “main branches” on a par with foundational theory.

Classification references: APS PhySH and arXiv Category Taxonomy (checked 2026-11-01). This is an English navigation scheme reorganized for learning, not an item-by-item translation or a complete mirror of the official classifications; research fields, physical systems, techniques, and related disciplines are different dimensions and overlap one another.

The same topic can have more than one home: quantum optics links optics with atomic physics; magnetic reconnection links space physics with plasma physics; active soft matter links condensed matter with biophysics. Multiple entry points are used here to express these relations.

What still cannot be claimed as covered

Fine-grained techniques, specific materials, particular astronomical objects, and interdisciplinary directions that keep appearing cannot be exhausted by a fixed catalog. String theory, quantum gravity, and some schemes beyond the Standard Model are candidate theories; they are not mixed in with experimentally confirmed conclusions. When frontier classifications are updated, the official subject schemes should be rechecked.

Neutrino Oscillation

Goal: build the model by following the derivation, compute a new case on your own, and point out when the approximation fails.

Prerequisites: the concept of the weak interaction; quantum superposition. If the prerequisites are unfamiliar, first return to Mathematics preparation. The two introductory lessons in this field do not amount to satisfying all advanced prerequisites.

The problem and the physical picture

The three generations of neutrinos convert into one another during propagation because the mass eigenstates do not coincide with the weak-interaction eigenstates; solar, atmospheric, and reactor experiments have established the mass differences and mixing angles — neutrinos have mass, which goes beyond the minimal Standard Model.

P(ν_e→ν_μ)=sin²2θ·sin²(Δm²L/4E)

From assumptions to results

  1. The weak eigenstates are a unitary mixing of the mass eigenstates.
  2. The different masses make the phase difference accumulate during propagation ∝Δm²L/E.
  3. Long-baseline scans extract Δm² and θ.
  4. Solar neutrinos receive MSW matter-effect corrections.
  5. The picture: two mass differences (7.5×10⁻⁵ and 2.5×10⁻³ eV²) plus three mixing angles; the CP phase is still undetermined.

Fill in the intermediate algebraic steps on paper. When you cannot reproduce one, first point to the equation where you are stuck, then ask me.

Worked example and check

Super-K discovered in 1998 that atmospheric ν_μ traveling up through the Earth disappear; Daya Bay measured θ₁₃≈8.5° in 2012; the solar ν_e arrival rate of about 1/3–1/2 agrees with the MSW prediction (Nobel Prizes in 2002 and 2015).

Limits of validity

The absolute masses are unknown (β endpoint <0.8 eV); the Majorana nature and CP violation remain under measurement.

Independent computation

Show solution

Δm²₃₂≈2.5×10⁻³ eV².

Transfer and counterexample

Why does oscillation prove that neutrinos have mass?

Check the transfer-exercise solution

Oscillation requires distinct mass eigenstates (Δm²≠0); the massless neutrinos of the minimal SM would not oscillate.

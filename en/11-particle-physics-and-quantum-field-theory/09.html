The Holographic Principle and AdS/CFT

Goal: build the model by following the derivation, compute a new case on your own, and point out when the approximation fails.

Prerequisites: the concept of black holes; the language of quantum field theory. If the prerequisites are unfamiliar, first return to Mathematics preparation. The two introductory lessons in this field do not amount to satisfying all advanced prerequisites.

The problem and the physical picture

Black-hole entropy is proportional to the horizon area, suggesting that the degrees of freedom of gravity are encoded on a boundary of one lower dimension. AdS/CFT provides a concrete realization of the holographic principle: a gravitational theory in the bulk is equivalent to a conformal field theory on the boundary.

S_BH=kA/(4l_p²); AdS₅/CFT₄ duality

From assumptions to results

  1. Derive S∝A from the black-hole temperature and the first law of thermodynamics.
  2. The gravitational description and the gauge description of D3-branes match in the same parameter region, establishing the holographic dictionary.
  3. Boundary correlation functions are mapped to propagators in the bulk.

Fill in the intermediate algebraic steps on paper. When you cannot reproduce one, first point to the equation where you are stuck, then ask me.

Worked example and check

A class of isotropic strongly coupled holographic theories described by two-derivative Einstein gravity has η/s=ℏ/(4πk_B). This motivates quark–gluon plasma studies but is not a proved universal lower bound: higher-derivative or anisotropic models can deviate, and QCD is not identical to N=4 super-Yang–Mills theory.

Limits of validity

In the strictest sense the duality is mathematically defined only for asymptotically AdS spacetimes; extending it to the real universe remains active research.

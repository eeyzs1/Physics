Stage-by-stage requirements

  1. Introductory: explain concepts qualitatively, unify units, and solve simple model problems.
  2. Undergraduate core: study the field’s governing equations and experiments systematically and complete multi-chapter derivations and problem sets; the short lessons in this directory serve only as topic demonstrations.
  3. Advanced: fill in the mathematics and specialized tools, read the corresponding graduate textbooks, and reproduce typical models; topics not yet written remain listed as to be added.
  4. Research training: reproduce results, verify errors, read papers, and accept review; only then gradually attempt original problems.

Systematic textbook: David Tong: Quantum Field Theory. For cross-field prerequisites and milestones see the complete staged learning path.

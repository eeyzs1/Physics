Lattice Gauge Theory

Goal: build the model by following the derivation, compute a new case on your own, and point out when the approximation fails.

Prerequisites: the Standard Model; Monte Carlo methods. If the prerequisites are unfamiliar, first return to Mathematics preparation. The two introductory lessons in this field do not amount to satisfying all advanced prerequisites.

The problem and the physical picture

Discretizing spacetime into lattice sites and placing the gauge fields on the links gives QCD a non-perturbative definition. Perturbation theory fails in the strong-coupling region, and the lattice is the main tool for first-principles computations of the hadron spectrum and QCD phase transitions.

lattice spacing a→0 limit keeps g²(a)N fixed; Wilson loop criterion

From assumptions to results

  1. Connect neighboring lattice sites with the gauge-invariant link variables U_μ(x); the action is built from the smallest closed loops.
  2. The area law of the Wilson loop gives the criterion for quark confinement.
  3. Sample the partition function with Monte Carlo and extrapolate to the continuum limit.

Fill in the intermediate algebraic steps on paper. When you cannot reproduce one, first point to the equation where you are stuck, then ask me.

Worked example and check

A lattice spacing of 0.1 fm can resolve hadron spectra on scales of about 1 GeV; modern computations reach percent-level accuracy, supporting first-principles predictions of meson/nucleon spectra and hadronic matrix elements.

Limits of validity

The fermion-doubling problem requires improved actions; the signal-to-noise ratio worsens exponentially with volume; the results depend on the continuum-limit extrapolation.

Transfer and counterexample

Why are lattice computations performed in imaginary time?

Check the transfer-exercise solution

In imaginary time the partition function is a positive-definite weight that Monte Carlo can sample; real-time problems require analytic continuation.

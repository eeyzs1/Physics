Stage-by-stage requirements

  1. Introductory: be able to explain concepts qualitatively, make units consistent, and solve simple model problems.
  2. Undergraduate core: systematically study the field's governing equations and experiments, completing multi-chapter derivations and problem sets; the short lessons in this directory are only sample topics.
  3. Advanced: fill in mathematics and specialized tools, read the corresponding graduate textbooks, and reproduce representative models; topics not yet written remain listed as to be added.
  4. Research training: reproduce results, verify errors, read papers, and accept review; only then gradually attempt original problems.

Systematic textbook: David Tong: mathematical-biology. Cross-field prerequisites and milestones are in the full tiered path.

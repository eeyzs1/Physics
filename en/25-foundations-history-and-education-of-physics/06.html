Big science and open science

Goal: follow the derivation to build the model, compute a new case independently, and point out when the approximation fails.

Prerequisites: scientific method; communication. If a prerequisite is unfamiliar, first go back to Mathematics preparation. The two introductory lessons in this branch do not by themselves satisfy every advanced prerequisite.

Problem and physical picture

The scale of modern physics has changed how research is done: kilometer-scale facilities, thousand-member collaborations, and global data networks; peer review, preregistration, open data, and reproducible workflows have become the social tools of quality control.

authors per paper in a large collaboration ~10³ (empirical, particle physics)

From assumptions to results

  1. The incentives and organization of knowledge production generate measurable aggregate statistics.
  2. Policy (budgets, degree of openness) changes how the system responds.
  3. Methodologically, this is the mirror image of a physics experiment.

A single LHC collaboration paper has more than 5000 authors; the GW170817 gravitational-wave–electromagnetic multi-messenger joint campaign mobilized a global network — large-scale collaboration is now the norm.

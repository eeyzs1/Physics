Rovibrational Spectra of Molecules: The P and R Branches

Goal: build the model by following the derivation, compute a new case on your own, and point out when the approximations break down.

Prerequisites: the molecular energy scales lesson; the concept of selection rules. If the prerequisites are unfamiliar, go back to Mathematics preparation first. The two introductory lessons in the same field do not amount to meeting all advanced prerequisites.

Problem and physical picture

Rovibrational transitions split by ΔJ=±1 into an R branch (high-frequency side) and a P branch (low-frequency side); the lines are evenly spaced by 2B, with the Q branch missing at the center — a standard tool for inferring bond lengths and force constants from infrared spectra.

Ē_J=ℏ²J(J+1)/(2I); R branch ν=ν₀+2B(J+1)

From assumptions to results

  1. Rotational and vibrational levels are approximately additive (centrifugal distortion neglected).
  2. Electric-dipole transitions have ΔJ=±1, so the two sets of frequencies ν₀±2B(J+1) are evenly spaced.
  3. Invert the rotational constant B=h/(8π²cI) to get the moment of inertia, then use I=μr² to get the bond length.

Please fill in the intermediate algebra steps on paper. If you cannot reproduce a step, first point out the equation where you got stuck, then ask me.

Example and check

For CO, B≈1.93 cm⁻¹: adjacent lines are spaced by 2B≈3.86 cm⁻¹; I=h/(8π²cB)≈1.45×10⁻⁴⁶ kg·m², and with a reduced mass of 1.14×10⁻²⁶ kg this gives a bond length of about 0.113 nm, consistent with the known value.

Range of applicability

Rigid-rotor and harmonic approximations; centrifugal distortion at high J makes the lines crowd together slightly.

Independent calculation

Show solution

ν=ν₀+2B(J+1); adjacent J values differ by 2B.

Transfer and counterexample

Why do rovibrational spectra usually lack a Q branch with ΔJ=0?

Compare with the transfer solution

In a parallel transition the dipole lies along the molecular axis, and angular momentum conservation forbids ΔJ=0; perpendicular bands (e.g. NO) can have a Q branch.

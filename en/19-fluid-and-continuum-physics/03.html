Turbulence and the energy cascade

Goal: follow the derivation to build the model, compute a new case independently, and point out when the approximation fails.

Prerequisites: Reynolds number; dimensional analysis. If a prerequisite is unfamiliar, first go back to Mathematics preparation. The two introductory lessons in this branch do not by themselves satisfy every advanced prerequisite.

The problem and the physical picture

The Reynolds number measures the ratio of inertia to viscosity. Beyond a critical value, laminar flow destabilizes and multiscale eddies appear; energy passes step by step from large eddies to small ones until it is dissipated by viscosity — this is the root of the difficulty of predicting flow mixing and drag.

Re=vL/ν; inertial-range energy spectrum E(k)∝k^(−5/3)

From assumptions to results

  1. Nondimensionalizing the Navier–Stokes equations gives Re.
  2. At large Re, vortex stretching continually generates ever smaller scales.
  3. The smallest (Kolmogorov) scale is fixed by balancing viscous dissipation, giving the estimate η∝L·Re^(−3/4).

Fill in the intermediate algebra on paper. If you cannot reproduce a step, first pinpoint the equation where you get stuck, then ask me.

Worked example and check

Transition in a water pipe occurs at about Re≈2300; with Re=10⁶ and L=1 m, η≈L×10^(−4.5)≈L/32000≈3×10⁻⁵ m (about 30 μm), roughly five orders of magnitude smaller than the macroscopic scale.

Limits of validity

This is the picture of a statistically stationary, isotropic cascade; there is still no universal predictive theory for wall turbulence or for the location of transition.

Independent computation

Show solution

Re=vL/ν=1×1/10⁻⁶=10⁶.

Transfer and counterexample

In geometrically similar flows with Re held fixed, how does the Kolmogorov scale change with L?

Compare with the transfer-exercise solution

η∝L; it scales up in geometric similarity.

Advanced self-test

This one adds a step beyond the previous problem or requires a unit conversion; on the days 1, 3, and 7 reviews, do this problem instead, to avoid memorizing answers.

Show solution

Re^(3/4)=10³, so η/L≈10⁻³.

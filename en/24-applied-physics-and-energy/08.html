Semiconductor Device Physics: From Diodes to Transistors

Goal: follow the derivation to build the model, independently compute a new case, and point out when the approximation fails.

Prerequisites: the p–n junction lesson; the effective-mass lesson. If the prerequisites are unfamiliar, first return to Mathematics preparation. The two introductory lessons in this field do not amount to satisfying all advanced prerequisites.

The problem and the physical picture

Stack the physics of doping and junctions into devices: LEDs and lasers (light emitted by recombination under forward bias), MOSFETs (gate voltage controlling the channel), CMOS logic and memory — the physical foundation of Moore's law's sixty years, and the 60 mV/dec thermodynamic shackle.

subthreshold swing S=ln10·k_BT/q≈60 mV/dec

From assumptions to results

  1. The MOSFET's gate field modulates the channel carriers; below threshold the conductance varies exponentially, with a room-temperature limiting swing of 60 mV/dec (set by the thermal carrier distribution k_BT/q).
  2. Short-channel effects and drain-induced barrier lowering challenge scaling.
  3. Tunnel FETs and negative-capacitance FETs attempt to break below 60 mV.
  4. An LED's band gap directly determines its emission wavelength (the inverse process of the photovoltaic lesson).

Fill in the intermediate algebraic steps on paper. If you cannot reproduce one, first point out the equation where you got stuck, then ask me about it.

Worked example and check

At the 5 nm node, FinFET/GAA structures maintain electrostatic control; GaN power devices have a breakdown field 10× higher; the hard 60 mV/dec room-temperature subthreshold-swing limit drives research into low-dimensional and tunneling devices.

Range of applicability

Ideality factors and leakage current make real values larger; new mechanisms (tunneling, ferroelectrics) are challenging the limit.

Transfer and counterexample

Why is an LED's color determined by the band gap?

Compare with the transfer-exercise solution

Electrons and holes injected under forward bias recombine at the band edges, so the photon energy≈band gap (light emission is the inverse of photovoltaics).

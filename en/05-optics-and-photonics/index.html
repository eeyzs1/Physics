Stage-by-stage requirements

  1. Introductory: explain concepts qualitatively, work in consistent units, and solve simple model problems.
  2. Undergraduate core: study the field's governing equations and experiments systematically, completing multi-chapter derivations and problem sets; the mini-lessons in this directory are only demonstrations of selected topics.
  3. Advanced: fill in the mathematics and specialized tools, read the corresponding graduate textbooks, and reproduce representative models; topics not yet written remain listed as pending.
  4. Research training: reproduce results, validate error estimates, read papers, and accept critique; only then gradually attempt original problems.

Systematic textbooks: David Tong: catalog of physics lecture notes by level. For cross-field prerequisites and milestones, see the complete staged path.

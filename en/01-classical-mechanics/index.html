Stage-by-stage requirements

  1. Introductory: explain concepts qualitatively, work with consistent units, and solve simple model problems.
  2. Undergraduate core: study the field's governing equations and experiments systematically, completing multi-chapter derivations and problem sets; the short lessons in this directory are only demonstrations of selected topics.
  3. Advanced: fill gaps in mathematics and specialized tools, read the corresponding graduate textbooks, and reproduce representative models; topics not yet written remain listed as to be filled in.
  4. Research training: reproduce results, verify error estimates, read papers, and accept critique; only afterwards gradually attempt original problems.

Systematic textbook: David Tong: Classical Dynamics. For cross-field prerequisites and milestones, see the complete graded path.

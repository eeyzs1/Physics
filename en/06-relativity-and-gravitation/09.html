The Classical Tests of General Relativity

Goal: build the model by following the derivation, compute a new case independently, and point out when the approximation breaks down.

Prerequisites: the equivalence principle; spacetime diagrams. If the prerequisites feel shaky, first return to Mathematics preparation. The two introductory lessons in this field do not by themselves satisfy every advanced prerequisite.

Problem and physical picture

GR has four classical tests: gravitational redshift, the deflection of light, the perihelion precession of Mercury, and radar echo delay; gravitational waves and imaging of black holes are modern extensions. The weak-field expansion yields differences that discriminate between GR and Newtonian theory.

Deflection α=4GM/(c²b)；precession Δφ=6πGM/[c²a(1−e²)]

From assumptions to results

  1. The weak-field linearized equations are solved for the first-order corrections to light rays and orbits.
  2. Counting only the equivalence principle gives half the deflection (the Newtonian value), and spatial curvature supplies the other half.
  3. The residual precession of 43 arcseconds per century is explained exactly.

Fill in the intermediate algebra steps on paper. If you cannot reproduce them, first point to the equation where you got stuck, then ask me.

Stuck? Expand the key intermediate steps
  1. Linearize about g=η+h; in the harmonic gauge, □h̄_μν=−(16πG/c⁴)T_μν.
  2. The exterior solution of a point mass gives h_00 of order 2GM/(c²r) and an anisotropic h_ij.
  3. Light rays follow null geodesics: the equivalence principle (h_00) contributes half the deflection and spatial curvature (h_ij) the other half, combining to α=4GM/(c²b).
  4. Perihelion precession comes from the 1/r³ correction to the potential, 43″ per century.
  5. Radar delay accumulates the slowing of the coordinate speed of light over the round trip; at the solar limb it is of order 200 μs.

If you are still stuck, write down the specific equation and then discuss it; that works better than rereading repeatedly.

Example and check

Light grazing the solar limb is deflected by 1.75 arcseconds (twice the Newtonian value of 0.87); the 1919 solar eclipse observations came down on the side of GR; Shapiro radar delay (a round-trip delay of order 200 μs for signals grazing the solar limb) and gravitational redshift were verified one by one afterward.

Range of validity

Weak-field tests; strong fields and dynamical spacetimes are tested by gravitational-wave detection and black hole imaging.

Independent calculation

Show solution

4GM/(c²b)≈1.75″, exactly twice the Newtonian value.

Transfer and counterexample

Why is “the deflection being twice the Newtonian value” a decisive criterion?

Check the transfer solution

The equivalence principle alone gives 0.87″, and spatial curvature doubles it; the experiment chooses between the two theories.

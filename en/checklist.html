Check-off criteria (all four required)

  1. Without looking at the lesson text, state in your own words what the model describes and what each quantity in the formula is.

  3. State the conditions of validity of the formula and give one scenario in which the model fails.
  4. Complete the transfer exercise independently; if you get stuck on any step, uncheck the item, go back to the lesson page to review, and come back.

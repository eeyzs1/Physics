Stage-by-stage requirements

  1. Introductory: be able to explain concepts qualitatively, put quantities in consistent units, and solve simple model problems.
  2. Undergraduate core: study the field's governing equations and experiments systematically, completing multi-chapter derivations and problem sets; the short lessons in this directory are only demonstrations of selected topics.
  3. Advanced: fill in the mathematics and specialized tools, read the corresponding graduate textbooks, and reproduce typical models; topics not yet written remain listed as to be added.
  4. Research training: reproduce results, verify error bars, read papers, and accept review; only afterwards gradually attempt original problems.

Systematic textbook: David Tong: topics-in-quantum-mechanics. For cross-branch prerequisites and milestones see the Full graded path.

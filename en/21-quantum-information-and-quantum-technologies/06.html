Quantum teleportation and the no-cloning theorem

Goal: follow the derivations to build the model, compute a new case independently, and point out when the approximation fails.

Prerequisites: the entanglement lesson; quantum gates. If a prerequisite feels shaky, first go back to Mathematics preparation. The two introductory lessons in this branch do not by themselves satisfy all advanced prerequisites.

The problem and the physical picture

Teleportation uses one entangled pair plus two classical bits to "move" an unknown quantum state to a distant site — it neither copies nor travels faster than light; the no-cloning theorem is the cornerstone of quantum information security: linear quantum mechanics forbids perfect copying of an arbitrary unknown state.

teleportation：|ψ〉+Bell measurement+2 classical bits+X/Z correction

From assumptions to results

  1. Assume a cloning machine U|ψ〉|0〉=|ψ〉|ψ〉 that works for |ψ〉=|0〉 and |1〉 individually; then for the superposition (|0〉+|1〉)/√2, linearity forces U's output to be the entangled state (|00〉+|11〉)/√2, whereas a true copy should be the separable product state (|00〉+|01〉+|10〉+|11〉)/2 — linearity makes the two contradictory, so no cloner exists.
  2. Teleportation therefore "moves" the state using entanglement plus a measurement that destroys the original.

Fill in the intermediate algebraic steps on paper. If you cannot reproduce one, first point out the equation where you are stuck, then ask me.

Worked examples and checks

Alice's Bell measurement yields one of 4 outcomes (each with probability 1/4), which she reports to Bob classically; Bob applies I/X/Z/XZ according to 00/01/10/11 to recover |ψ〉; this has been demonstrated over hundred-kilometer-scale optical fiber and satellite links (Micius), and is used in quantum repeater research.

Limits of applicability

Ideal entanglement and complete measurements; in noisy channels the teleportation fidelity drops, and it becomes practically useful only when combined with error correction/repeaters.

Transfer and counterexamples

Since teleportation is "instantaneous", why doesn't it violate relativity?

Compare with the transfer solution

Until the 2 classical bits arrive, Bob holds only the maximally mixed state, which carries no information; the speed of information is limited to the speed of light by the classical channel.

Alfvén waves in magnetohydrodynamics

Goal: build the model by following the derivation, compute a new case independently, and point out when the approximation fails.

Prerequisites: magnetic fields; fluid density; wave equations. If a prerequisite is shaky, first return to Mathematics preparation. The two introductory lessons in this field do not by themselves satisfy all advanced prerequisites.

Problem and physical picture

The MHD approximation treats the ensemble of charged particles as a conducting continuum. The transverse bending of field lines provides the restoring action, and the fluid mass provides the inertia; together they set the Alfvén speed.

v_A=B₀/√(μ₀ρ)

From assumptions to result

  1. Linearize the momentum equation and the ideal induction equation about a uniform background.
  2. Eliminating the magnetic perturbation gives a wave equation for the transverse displacement, with squared speed B₀²/(μ₀ρ).

Limits of validity

Long-wavelength, low-frequency, ideal MHD, small perturbations of a uniform background; kinetic scales and dissipative effects can break the approximation.

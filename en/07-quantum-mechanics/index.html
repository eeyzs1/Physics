Stage-by-stage requirements

  1. Introductory: explain concepts qualitatively, use consistent units, and solve simple model problems.
  2. Undergraduate core: study the field's governing equations and experiments systematically and work through multi-chapter derivations and problem sets; the mini-lessons in this directory are demonstrations of selected topics only.
  3. Advanced: fill in the mathematics and specialized tools, read the corresponding graduate textbooks, and reproduce typical models; topics not yet written remain listed as to be added.
  4. Research training: reproduce results, verify their errors, read papers, and accept review; only then begin attempting original problems step by step.

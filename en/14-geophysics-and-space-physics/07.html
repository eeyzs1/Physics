Paleomagnetism and the Geodynamo

Goal: build the model by following the derivation, independently compute a new case, and point out when the approximation fails.

Prerequisites: the plate-tectonics lesson; magnetic-field concepts. If the prerequisites are unfamiliar, first return to Mathematics preparation. The two introductory lessons in the same field do not amount to satisfying all advanced prerequisites.

The problem and the physical picture

Rocks lock in the geomagnetic direction at the time they cool: marine magnetic stripes record the history of reversals, and continental paleomagnetic data reconstruct continental drift; the energy source of the geomagnetic field is a convective dynamo in the liquid iron of the outer core, and the magnetic poles reverse on average every few hundred thousand years.

VADM≈8×10²² A·m²; mean reversal interval ~3×10⁵ years

From assumptions to results

  1. The magnetohydrodynamic dynamo equations (the induction equation) sustain a dipole field under outer-core convection.
  2. Paleomagnetism measures the remanent-magnetization directions of lavas and sediments and derives polar-wander paths and reversal frequencies statistically.
  3. During a reversal the intensity drops to about 20%, lasting on the order of a thousand years.
  4. The geomagnetic north pole is currently moving toward Siberia at about 50 km/yr.

Example and check

The Brunhes/Matsuyama reversal boundary (780,000 years ago) is a Quaternary stratigraphic marker; dating of oceanic-crust magnetic stripes established seafloor spreading (linked to the plate-tectonics lesson); geomagnetic storms and magnetospheric coupling are covered in the space-weather lesson.

Range of validity

Self-consistent dynamo simulations are still limited (the viscosity gap); the trigger mechanism of reversals is not settled.

Transfer and counterexample

Why can magnetic stripes in the oceanic crust determine the age of the seafloor?

Solution to the transfer problem

Basalt locks in the geomagnetic polarity when it cools; comparing with the calibrated reversal sequence yields the age, and the stripes are symmetric about the mid-ocean ridge.

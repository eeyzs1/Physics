Stage-by-stage requirements

  1. Introductory: be able to explain concepts qualitatively, make units consistent, and solve simple model problems.
  2. Undergraduate core: study the field's governing equations and experiments systematically and complete multi-chapter derivations and problem sets; the short lessons in this directory are only sample topics.
  3. Advanced: fill in mathematics and specialized tools, read the corresponding graduate textbooks, and reproduce representative models; topics not yet written remain listed as to be added.
  4. Research training: reproduce results, validate errors, read papers, and accept critique; only afterwards gradually attempt original problems.

Systematic textbooks: MIT OpenCourseWare: physics course search. Cross-field prerequisites and milestones are in the complete staged path.

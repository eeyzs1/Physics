Stage-by-Stage Requirements

  1. Introductory: be able to explain concepts qualitatively, put units on a common footing, and solve simple model problems.
  2. Undergraduate core: study the field's governing equations and experiments systematically and complete multi-chapter derivations and problem sets; the short lessons in this directory are only sample topics.
  3. Advanced: shore up the mathematics and specialized tools, read the corresponding graduate textbooks, and reproduce typical models; topics not yet written remain listed as to be filled in.
  4. Research training: reproduce results, verify errors, read papers, and accept review; only afterwards gradually attempt original problems.

Systematic textbook: David Tong: topics-in-quantum-mechanics. For cross-branch prerequisites and milestones, see the complete graded path.

Nuclear size and density saturation

Goal: follow the derivation to build the model, compute a new case independently, and point out when the approximation fails.

Prerequisites: the lesson on the liquid-drop model. If the prerequisites are unfamiliar, first go back to Mathematics preparation. The two introductory lessons in the same field do not amount to meeting all advanced prerequisites.

Problem and physical picture

The nuclear radius is R≈1.2·A^(1/3) fm: the volume is proportional to the number of nucleons and the nuclear matter density is nearly constant — direct evidence that the nuclear force is short-ranged and saturating, and the origin of the volume term in the liquid-drop model.

R=1.2·A^(1/3) fm; ρ₀≈0.16 fm⁻³

From assumptions to results

  1. Diffraction fringes in high-energy electron scattering measure a charge radius ∝A^(1/3).
  2. Density saturation means each nucleon interacts only with its nearest neighbors.
  3. From 3A/(4πR³), the nuclear matter density is about 0.16 fm⁻³, corresponding to a mass density of about 2.3×10¹⁷ kg/m³ (the order of magnitude of neutron-star matter).

Fill in the intermediate algebra steps on paper. If you cannot reproduce one, first point to the equation where you are stuck, then ask me.

Example and check

Lead-208: R≈1.2×5.93≈7.1 fm (experiment gives about 6.6–7.1 fm); a spoonful of nuclear matter weighs about as much as a mountain — the source of the surface gravity of neutron stars (link to the astrophysics field).

Range of applicability

The charge radius and the matter radius differ; deformed nuclei and weakly bound exotic nuclei deviate from A^(1/3).

Independent calculation

Show solution

208^(1/3)≈5.93, R≈7.1 fm.

Transfer and counterexample

What property of the nuclear force does density saturation indicate?

Check the transfer exercise solution

Short range and saturation — the binding energy per nucleon is approximately constant instead of growing with A as for a long-range force.

Advanced self-test

This one takes an extra step or requires a unit conversion; switch to it when reviewing on days 1, 3, and 7 to avoid memorizing the answer.

Show solution

0.16×10⁴⁵=1.6×10⁴⁴ m⁻³.
